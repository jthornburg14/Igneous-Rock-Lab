Physical Geology 2001

Igneous Rock Identification Lab

In this lab you will learn how geologists classify igneous rocks using composition and texture, then practice identifying nine rock samples on your own.

Lab Objectives
✓Understand the two axes of igneous rock classification — composition (silica content) and texture (cooling rate)
✓Explain Bowen's Reaction Series and how it links magma temperature to mineral composition
✓Use the classification table to identify nine igneous rock samples from their observable properties
✓Connect igneous rock types to the tectonic settings where they form
Follow these steps in order
1
Read through the Interactive Lab Guide

Work through all five content sections before attempting to identify any samples. The guide covers igneous environments, composition, Bowen's Reaction Series, texture, and tectonic settings — each with a short check-your-understanding question. You must complete the Rock Profile (final section) as part of your submission.

🌋 Open Lab Guide →

ℹ️ Opens in a new tab. Work through all 7 sections and complete your two rock profiles before downloading your results.

2
Identify the nine rock samples

Use the Rock ID site to examine each sample. Click the observation prompts to gather information about color, texture, minerals, and special features. Fill out the worksheet, consult the built-in reference table, and submit your identification. You only get one submission per sample — work through all the observation prompts and the reference table before committing to an answer.

🔬 Open Rock ID Site →
How to use the reference table: Click the 📋 Reference tables tab at the top of the page. First determine color to establish composition (felsic / intermediate / mafic), then determine crystal size to establish texture (phaneritic / aphanitic / porphyritic / glassy / vesicular). Where those two intersect in the table is your rock name.

⚠️ Enter your name at the bottom of the page before you begin — it will appear on your downloaded results file.

3
Download and submit your results

When you have finished identifying all nine samples, click ⬇ Download results at the bottom of the Rock ID site. Then return to the Lab Guide and click ⬇ Download my work to save your quiz answers and rock profiles. Upload both files to this assignment in Canvas.
